Introduction

CreTAKE is a two-message authenticated key-exchange suite for endpoints with KEM-based or signature-based credentials. Four frameworks cover every ordered pairing of these credential types under a common interface.

  1. 01

    Complete credential coverage

    K2S, S2K, K2K, and S2S support both mixed and matching long-term credential types.

  2. 02

    Two-message exchange

    One message in each direction establishes a session key with authentication under the selected framework.

  3. 03

    Concrete instantiations

    The specification combines PolarLAC, ZEN, and BiT into 25 instances across three security levels.
