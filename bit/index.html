Introduction

BiT is a lattice-based digital signature scheme built on the Fiat–Shamir with Aborts paradigm. It combines parabolic masking with a bimodal structure and a separately parameterized parabolic target distribution to improve rejection sampling.

  1. 01

    Parabolic masking

    Masking vectors follow a bounded parabolic distribution, concentrating probability near the center to strengthen masking.

  2. 02

    Bimodal rejection

    Rejection sampling uses a parabolic target distribution with a separately chosen radius to remove dependence on the secret key.

  3. 03

    Compact representation

    Public-key compression and signature hints reduce the amount of data that needs to be transmitted.
